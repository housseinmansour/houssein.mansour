About & Research

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at ISAE-SUPAERO. My research lies at the intersection of probabilistic programming, program semantics, and Bayesian inference.

Advisors: Christine Tasson , Xavier Thirioux , and Raphaëlle Crubillé .

Bayesian Inference for Hybrid Models

Probabilistic programs define probability distributions. We study how these distributions can be represented and approximated algorithmically, especially when conditioning on observations makes exact inference difficult.

We are particularly interested in models combining discrete and continuous dynamics.

Contextual Distances in Randomized Programming

Contextual distance measures how well two randomized programs can be distinguished by program contexts. When contexts can copy their argument, small differences can be amplified.

Our work gives a probabilistic proof of this phenomenon and derives lower bounds for contextual distances when copying is restricted.
